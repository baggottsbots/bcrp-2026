Meet Our Chair

Kevin
Chairman, BCRP

Born and raised in the New York City area, Kevin has always been passionate about politics. He has held multiple party roles since 2017 — Membership Chair, Treasurer, and interim-Chairman — before being unanimously elected Chairman in 2020. During his tenure, the party has reached significant milestones in membership, fundraising, and electoral success.

  • Increased party membership by more than 400%
  • Grew party cash on hand by more than 300%
  • Recruited over 200 poll watchers and workers to monitor elections
  • Implemented upgraded technologies for volunteer training and phone banks
  • Republican candidates have won every competitive election for 5+ years
